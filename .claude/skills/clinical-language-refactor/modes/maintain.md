# Mode: maintain — writing new or revised teaching copy

Use it while authoring, so new copy starts in the approved voice instead of being refactored into
it later. Structure, sequencing and assessment design come from `medical-education-modules`; this
mode governs the wording.

- Before drafting, read the domain profile, the approved examples, the module's copy gate and its
  sources.
- Make the clinical term the primary label, follow it with a precise explanation, and add an
  analogy only when it helps. Don't coin a nickname or framework where an established concept
  exists, and keep the technical depth.
- Keep observation, interpretation, intervention and outcome in separate clauses, each with its
  certainty and source limits. Transcripts guide register, never treatment rules, numbers, device
  capabilities or diagnoses.
- Name the referent of every control and readout, and say whether it is set, measured,
  calculated, estimated or simulated. Reuse the canonical registries, message namespaces and stable
  IDs; change no model or scoring behavior.
- New cases keep the staged information design: nothing before the commit point names the
  diagnosis, mechanism or correct action; distractors stay plausible and balanced; every
  explanation matches the data on screen.
- Self-review with the regression cases and the module's checks. New terminology is `draft` until
  the owner approves it; report source gaps and E2 questions separately.
