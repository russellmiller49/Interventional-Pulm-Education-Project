Predict the route of airflow — PowerPoint asset pack

Recommended use:
1. Copy the two slides from predict-the-route-of-airflow.pptx into your deck.
2. Present the prompt slide, ask learners to commit to inspiration and expiration routes, then advance to the reveal slide.
3. Alternatively, insert airflow-route-predict-reveal.mp4 and set Playback > Start Automatically.

Static assets:
- airflow-route-prompt.png and airflow-route-reveal.png are 1920 × 1080.
- Matching SVG files are included for lossless scaling.

Clinical scope:
- Depicts a one-way speaking valve with a fully deflated cuff.
- Inspiration enters through the tracheostomy tube.
- Expiration travels around the tube, through the larynx, and out of the mouth and nose.
- Never use a one-way speaking valve with an inflated cuff.
