## Implementation brief

Reuse the shared StageLayout with existing fractions and pane floors; add an explicit five-unit policy in the MV feature. Render task-specific prerequisite teaching and a labeled worked demonstration before independent application. Withhold that task’s worked solution while answering. A captured actual breath supplies keyboard inspection without a timing requirement. Predictions remain first attempts; recorded observation answers add interpretation without prose-length or prediction-correctness gates.

Learning evidence distinguishes modeled values, queued/active maneuvers, acquired measurements with time/conditions/validity, and historical values. Retain version-1 storage/history; version-2 evidence applies to revised independent tasks. Final-check question scoring is unchanged.

This assignment supersedes the old local blanket post-prediction teaching rule for these five units only. The shared stage and later units keep their existing disclosure rules. The obsolete reflection-length gate and obsolete pane order remain retired.

The registered AARC 2024 Patient–Ventilator Assessment source (PMID 39048148; DOI 10.4187/respcare.12007) was checked in its [official full text](https://www.aarc.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/patient-ventilator-assessment-aarc-cpg.pdf), including PICO 1/Table 2 on settings/monitoring, expired VT, plateau, PEEP/auto-PEEP, driving pressure and oxygenation. New wording adds no treatment target and does not claim this is the latest guideline. Faculty/device review remains necessary for plateau/total-PEEP assumptions, recent-effort interpretation, breath-relative volume conventions, idealized VC/PC limits, model output variation and manufacturer terminology/fidelity.

Two pre-existing shared-shell limitations were observed and left explicit:

1. Scoped axe found zero violations in the selected mobile teaching panes after the local contrast/heading fixes. The three larger sizes retain one moderate `landmark-complementary-is-top-level` finding from the unchanged native console monitor `<aside>` inside another landmark. This is not an accessibility certification or a site-wide axe pass.
2. Rejecting **MV progress storage** is supported in memory. Artificially rejecting **all** `localStorage.getItem/setItem` calls crashes the unchanged shared `ThemeProvider` at `src/app/[locale]/layout.tsx:79` before the lesson mounts. Fixing that global provider is outside this authorized batch; do not describe complete site-storage denial as passing.
