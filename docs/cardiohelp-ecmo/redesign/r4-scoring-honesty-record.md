## Tests that encoded the defects, rewritten

- `engine.reducer.test.ts`: the differential and LV-loading cases now assert invariance and the
  cause credit; the bubble-reset case clamps both limbs before correcting.
- `clinical-response.test.ts`: the hemorrhage, tension and recirculation cases read the patient
  after one `STEP`, and assert the same-time invariance explicitly.

## What a learner sees differently

The gas panel and the console say "delivered below set flow" instead of naming an interrupted
source before commitment (I3d). The membrane's outlet saturation falls during a sweep-off trial.
The right arm stays low after "review the upper body" until the debrief explains why. A clamp or a
correction changes the circuit at once and the patient a second later. A wrong plan followed by
the right moves earns a debrief that says so, not a mastery line.

## Limitations still true

The model still simplifies every drainage-limited cause into one corrective action; the order of
the two isolation clamps is a protocol matter the reducer does not judge; the venous-estimate
target for the outlet saturation is a bounded-model statement, and the rate of two points per
second is authored, not measured.
