## Conclusions revealed by the matrix

- The pathway is conceptually cumulative, but its visible entry point bypasses all ten foundations. Several lessons therefore rely on pressure zones, provenance, and series/parallel physiology before the ordinary “Start curriculum” path introduces them.
- The two tracks intentionally repeat pressure, sweep, gas-source, bubble, and power mechanisms. The clinically meaningful difference is the reassessment obligation: VV emphasizes gas exchange and recirculation; VA adds native-heart contribution, territory-specific sampling, perfusion, and limb assessment.
