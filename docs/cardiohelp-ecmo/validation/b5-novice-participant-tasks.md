# ECMO console lab — participant task sheet

Thank you for helping us test this teaching module.

**You are not being tested. The module is.** There is no score, nothing is recorded against your
name, and there is no such thing as falling behind. If something is confusing, that is the single
most useful thing you can tell us — confusion here is a defect in what we built, not in you.

## How to work

Please **think aloud continuously**. Say what you are looking at, what you are reading, what you
expect to happen, and what surprises you. If you go quiet for a while, the person sitting with you
will ask "what are you looking at?" or "what do you expect to happen?" — that is only to keep the
narration going, never a hint that you have gone wrong.

You may use anything on the screen. You may change your mind at any point, out loud. If you get
stuck, say so and keep going — being stuck is a valid outcome and we want to see where it happens.

You can stop at any time, for any reason, without giving one.

Please do not enter any patient information, your own health information, or anything identifying
into the module. Nothing you type is needed for this session.

**Each task will already be open on the screen when it starts.** You do not need to find or choose
anything — the person sitting with you loads it and hands it over. Once it is in front of you, go
wherever you like in it.

---

## Before the tasks — finding your way

The module's front page is open. Nothing has been chosen yet.

**Tell us where you would start, and start there.** Then, without help, tell us three things: what
the screen is asking you to do right now, where you are in the whole sequence, and how you would
get to the next part when you are done. If you cannot find any of the three, say so — that is the
finding.

(The person sitting with you will note whether you reached the intended starting point unaided.
If you did not, the clinical tasks below are set aside for today and we talk about what got in the
way instead.)

---

## Task 1

A lesson is open in front of you. Do not touch any control yet.

Look at the readings on screen.

**Tell us which of these numbers you would believe right now and which you would not — and say why
for each one.**

---

## Task 2

A new lesson is open. Read what is in front of you.

The flow number on the display is rising. The saturation measured in the drainage line is rising.
The patient's own oxygenation is getting worse.

**Tell us what you think should happen to the pump speed here, and talk us through your reasoning.**

If the module offers you a choice to commit to, make the choice you actually believe, then carry on
and tell us whether what happens next matches what you expected.

---

## Task 3

A new lesson is open. Move forward through it until something changes.

**Tell us which part of this system you think has failed, and how you decided.**

---

## Task 4

A new lesson is open. Move forward through it until something changes.

The pump has stopped on its own.

**Tell us what that stop has achieved, and what you think has to happen before this circuit carries
blood again.** Talk through each thing you think has to happen, in the order you would do it.

If you want to try something on the controls, do it and tell us what you expected.

---

## Task 5

A new lesson is open.

You are shown a saturation from the right wrist, a saturation from the groin, and a reading taken
after the artificial lung. The right wrist reading is low. The other two look preserved.

**Tell us what each of those three readings represents.** Then tell us whether you think you could
change the situation from this console, and what makes you say that.

---

## Task 6

The window will be made smaller for this task.

Somewhere on screen there is a live signal from the machine, the task you are currently working on,
an explanation of what is being taught, and a way to ask the module to point out a control.

**Find all four, and tell us how you found each one.** Then tell us whether anything you had already
done or answered was lost along the way.

---

That is everything. Thank you — the next few minutes of questions are about your impressions, and
there are no wrong answers there either.
