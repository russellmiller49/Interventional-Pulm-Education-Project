## Cross-cutting observations

### Domain confusion — device vs circuit/gas vs patient

Did they attribute a signal to the wrong domain (e.g. treat an off-console arterial or blood-gas
value as a machine reading, or a gas-path failure as a pump problem)? Quote the moment.

>

### Displayed vs effective support

Did they treat the displayed flow number as the support the patient was receiving? Where did that
show up?

>

### Signal provenance

Did they ask, or wrongly assume, where a number came from (console channel / arterial line / blood
gas / pulse oximeter / computed)?

>

### Critical misunderstanding

Any confidently-held belief that would be unsafe at a bedside. Record verbatim, and record whether
it was corrected at debrief.

>

- [ ] None observed
- [ ] Observed and corrected at debrief
- [ ] Observed, correction incomplete — escalate
