# EBUS guided course — implementation review

Development delivery at `/en/ebus-guided`, with `/learn?section=acoustic-contact`, `/practice` and `/assess`. The course has 22 lessons, nine required labs, thirteen ordering/matching activities, eight final cases and three optional practice cases. Estimated study time is 162 guided minutes plus 25 minutes of final cases. This is formative education for early pulmonary fellows; completion is not a passing standard or a finding of procedural competence.

## Demonstrated learner journey

The browser starts with an empty course record and uses the real lesson route, controls, handlers and rendered embedded workbenches. It completes all 22 lessons in canonical order, all nine required labs, all thirteen ordering/matching activities and all eight case debriefs. The resulting record contains 22 completed lessons, eight completed cases and 90 original responses. Assessment completion survives reload. No legacy EBUS storage keys are created. The journey does not seed completion or send synthetic bridge observations.

Each lesson exposes orientation and prerequisite recall, a worked example, a committed prediction, an actual activity, an observation question, a clinical explanation and a changed-context transfer question. First decisions remain unchanged after retries. Ordinary Learn feedback follows submission and requires a separate Continue action. Practice and Assess withhold correctness until debrief. Unsafe choices receive immediate explanation and require revision. Final cases have no passing threshold.

The coupling pilot exposed an incorrect causal assumption: an assisted station preset maintained wall contact during rotation. The guided coupling activity now starts in free-drive at the existing station 7 centerline position and uses tip flexion. Browser-observed model contact increased from approximately 0.447 at the initial position to 0.896 at 10 degrees, with tissue echoes returning in the actual acoustic renderer. These are model indices, not measured pressure or clinical targets. The engine, geometry, calibration and renderer were not altered to make the activity pass.

The installed structured-module `scripts/audit-stage.mjs` was run against the actual coupling lesson. Its initial report was **FAIL**: it sampled SSR/early hydration before the workspace's ResizeObserver changed percentage widths to pixels, so compact panes appeared absent at phone widths. The failure was investigated, not suppressed. The feature-specific browser review waits for the shared workspace's measured pixel layout, then reproduces pane switching, checks real bounding overflow, exercises the lab and captures the hydrated states. No shared-stage changes or permissive replacement expectations were introduced. Running the unchanged installed smoke script against the completed production build then passed the entire viewport matrix; its separate report is `stage-audit-production/layout-smoke-report.json`. The real responsive/activity script also passed against that standalone build.

A 750 CSS-pixel viewport checked reflow equivalent to a 1500-pixel display at 200%. It had zero horizontal overflow. This is **not** a claim of testing browser chrome zoom at 200%. Physical mobile devices, Safari and an assistive-technology user session were not tested.

## Remaining release review

Faculty review of clinical wording, item/rationale pairing, current guideline interpretation and the model-to-patient limits remains pending. EUS-B is introductory cognitive teaching, not an esophageal navigation or needle simulator. Needle passage, tactile feedback, sedation, ventilation and complications are taught through cases/ordering tasks and are not physically modeled. Device-specific mechanical details and medication decisions defer to the current IFU and local protocols.

The implemented boundary is a separate, direct-link development course with reusable existing EBUS tools and independent educational progress. Public launch, replacement of the old EBUS course, localization review, learner timing/piloting and clinical sign-off are outside this delivery.
