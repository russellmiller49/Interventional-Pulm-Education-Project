# HD-01 — question ledger (ICU Hemodynamics)

Per-activity disposition ledger for every question-like surface on the public ICU Hemodynamics routes, drafted 2026-09-14 for the HD-01 self-paced conversion. Basis: the module content in worktree `claude-hd-01`, branch `claude/hd-01`, cut from `origin/main` at `5d21844f`. Item IDs, keys, plausibility grades and review status are read from the registries (`content/stageItems.ts`, `content/pacLearningItems.ts`, `content/pawpCaptureSequence.ts`, `content/pacAdvancementReasoning.ts`, `content/normalWaveformValidityChallenges.ts`, `content/questionSort.ts`, `content/storyProblems.ts`, `content/derivedMeasurementEpisodes.ts`, `content/cardiacOutputComparisons.ts`, `content/cases.ts`, `components/CardiacOutputMethodModel.tsx`, `components/WaveformRecognitionDrill.tsx`, `content/introductoryTeaching.ts`, `components/HemodynamicCaseActivity.tsx`). Nothing here is a clinical approval.

**What HD-01 does to every activity (mechanics, not a per-item decision).** Each question is optional reinforcement: the explanation (or reasoning, or worked sort, or labels) opens before any answer and records nothing; a checked answer gets the existing verdict; **Try again** clears it; **Continue** never waits for an answer, a correct answer, a count or a first attempt. No first-answer record, correct count, assisted tally or completion mark depends on correctness. The `Disposition` column records the _content_ recommendation on top of that.

**Dispositions.**

- `keep` — the item names a real clinical distinction and its alternatives are plausible confusions.
- `rewritten (HD-01)` — changed in this PR: wording or structure only, key and clinical meaning unchanged, item `draft` pending hemodynamics faculty review. Before/after text below.
- `rewrite (proposed)` — wording or grading worth changing, not changed here; pending faculty.
- `replace (proposed)` — the interaction would teach better as an annotated example or comparison; assigned to a named later slice.
- `combine (proposed)` — the item repeats a neighbouring activity.
- `remove` — none. Every activity names a concept.

**Counts (55 public activities).** rewritten (HD-01) 7 · keep 42 · rewrite (proposed) 2 · combine (proposed) 3 · replace (proposed) 1 · remove 0. The changed batch is 7 items, under the prompt's limit of ten. Dormant registries not rendered on a public route are listed at the end and are not counted.
