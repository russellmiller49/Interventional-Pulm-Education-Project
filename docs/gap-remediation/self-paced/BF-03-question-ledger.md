**Every disposition is a proposal, not an approval.** No item's key, plausibility grade, rationale, source reference, review item or `draft` status changed in BF-03. The only wording changes are in the closing section (batch 3), and they align what the course says about itself with the self-paced record; they change no clinical claim. Stems, options, situations and rationales are deliberately not quoted here; read them in the section files.

**How batches 1 and 2 were reviewed.** Two read-only review agents each took one batch of ten, chosen by safety consequence (airway and ventilation deterioration, bleeding, an instrument in an artificial airway, sedation and monitoring, loss of view, accessories). For each item they read the section file, compared the check with the teaching blocks in the `COURSE_FLOWS` chunks before it (`content/courseFlow.ts`), and cross-checked the BF-01 claim queue `surfaces` and the review register ids. They did not run the course. Their dispositions were then checked against the brief by the preparing assistant; no disposition was changed. Batch 3 was reviewed directly.

**Mechanics are not a per-item decision.** Since BF-01 each question is optional: explanation before answering, Try again, Continue without an answer, nothing recorded. An unsafe choice triggers the immediate safety alert only when the option is graded `unsafe`; that is why a grade that disagrees with its rationale is listed as a finding below.

**Legend.** Mix = option plausibility counts, best / reasonable-but-incomplete / incorrect-mechanism / unsafe. Surface: a section prediction is step `<section>-flow-v1-check`, a transfer is `<section>-flow-v1-transfer`, a practice case is `/practice?case=<id>`. "Recall-only" = restates blocks taught in the chunks just before it.

## The four combine proposals, and why BF-03 did not implement them

`Q27`, `Q26`, `Q19` and `Q04` restate what the chunks just before them taught. Each combine would remove the `check` chunk from that section's `COURSE_FLOWS` entry, keep the item authored but unrendered (as `five-controls-transfer` already is), and, where the check adds a contrast, carry that contrast into the adjacent activity:

| item  | chunk to remove                                     | resulting order                                                                      | contrast to carry                                                                                    |

| `Q27` | `COURSE_FLOWS['bleeding-priorities']` → `check`     | `baseline`, `position-and-priorities`, `application`, `review`, `transfer`           | suction to clear the view → frame `wedge-holding` feedback (clinical wording)                        |
| `Q26` | `COURSE_FLOWS['scope-in-a-tube']` → `check`         | `geometry`, `application`, `changed-tube`, `review`, `transfer`                      | optional: less space still matters without being measurable → `changed-tube` instruction             |
| `Q19` | `COURSE_FLOWS['sedation-and-monitoring']` → `check` | `monitoring`, `medication-record`, `application`, `review`, `transfer`               | two wrong models → ledger total or row feedback (clinical wording on a BF-01-C01 held surface)       |
| `Q04` | `COURSE_FLOWS['view-loss']` → `check`               | `normal-view`, `recovery`, `application`, `lens`, `exceptions`, `review`, `transfer` | suction and advance → lab feedback or an optional prediction before the lab; bleeding → `exceptions` |

They were not implemented because removing a section's `check` step is not a flow-only change:

- `bronchStageLesson` sets `predictionStepIndex` from the first round-0 prediction step. With the check removed it becomes −1 (or would need to point at another step).
- `predictionStepIndex` feeds the shared lesson stage, which this module may not edit: `learning-module/stage/stageModel.ts` hides steps after it until the prediction is committed, and `StageTeachingScope` hides after-commitment teaching on the same condition. In Foundations, `BronchStageHost` opens teaching on `predictionCommitted || confirmed >= predictionStepIndex`, and `engine/stageSession.ts` derives `predictionCommitted` from that step.
- `validateBronchStageLessons` requires a prediction step before the transfer, and `stage-precommit-leak.rendered.test.tsx` applies each section's deny patterns to the round-0 question.
- Two of the four carry clinical wording (`Q27`, `Q19`), and `Q19` sits on a held claim.

A later slice should decide, with faculty, which of the four to combine. It would then give Foundations a module-local rule for a section without a first check: which step opens after-commitment teaching, and how the validator and leak test treat it, without changing the shared stage. It should test disclosure in each affected section and walk the section in the browser. The same rule would unblock BF-01's `N03` proposal.
