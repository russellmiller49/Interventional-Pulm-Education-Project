## 3. IMPORTANT NON-BLOCKERS

### C01 — 3D target/tool label collision in ordinary supported layouts

- **Reproduction:** open `/en/peripheral-imaging/learn?section=projection`; let the first demonstration load; inspect the 3D scene's default Beam view. “Tool tip” paints over the beginning of “Authored target.” Side and Target camera presets still have overlapping text bounds in the inspected state.
- **Affected conditions:** all seven normal/text-enlargement matrix conditions, including 1440, 1024, 390 and 320px; also native 200% browser zoom. At 1440px the text rectangles overlap by approximately **30.7 × 12px**. Both labels are inside the scene: containment alone had missed their collision.
- **Learner impact:** loses a clear, separately readable object label in the orientation scene. The monitor explicitly identifies “Amber contour: authored target · white mark: tool tip”; teaching explains the fixed tool/lesion relationship, the projection and depth readouts remain available, and the actual controls work. No task requires selecting the occluded label. This is degraded orientation, not loss of the only required explanation or control.
- **Scope/ownership:** **PI-local 3D-suite presentation**, particularly `components/suite/views/ProjectionView3D.tsx` and `suite-scene.module.css`. It is not Device Intelligence or the shared learning-module shell. Similar label components exist in other PI suite views; their every camera/state combination was not exhaustively audited.
- **Severity:** **IMPORTANT NON-BLOCKER**.
- **Smallest useful repair:** give projected labels distinct anchors/leader lines or collision-aware screen offsets within the PI suite; check text-to-text overlap as well as scene containment. Preserve physical geometry, annotation meaning and simulation behavior.
- **Evidence:** `matrix.json`, `details.json`, `scene-1440-normal-projection.png`, `camera-*-default.png`, `native-zoom-cdp-2.png`.

### C03 — Non-stage pages require horizontal panning with enlarged narrow text

- **Reproduction:** open the PI hub, Learn landing, Practice landing or integrated-case landing at 320 × 740; set root text to 200%. Inspect the module title, release badge, navigation and long headings. Repeat at 390px/200%.
- **Affected conditions:** 320px/200% document widths were **443 / 416 / 416 / 469px** respectively. At 390px/200%, all four landing documents also exceeded the viewport. The four titles in the navigation crowd or overlap adjacent cells; this is additional to C04's secondary-description ellipsis. Normal text at 320/390px and the two inspected Learn lesson pages do not have this document overflow.
- **Learner impact:** extra horizontal panning and poorer visual separation of destinations. Overflowing headings are reachable by panning; all four keyboard link focus positions remained reachable, visibly focused and unobstructed, and Enter opened integrated cases. A 149px horizontal pan on the integrated landing exposed the right edge. Case answers, feedback, explanation and retry remained operable at 320/200%. No unavailable required interaction was demonstrated.
- **Scope/ownership:** **shared `learning-module-v2` frame/navigation plus PI's non-stage host/heading presentation**. The measured width should not all be attributed to the nav-description rule. This lies outside PI-WRAP-01's lesson-stage repair.
- **Severity:** **IMPORTANT NON-BLOCKER**, with shared ownership.
- **Smallest useful repair:** permit module identity/headings and release text to wrap, remove intrinsic-width pressure, and use a single-column or otherwise adequately sized navigation layout when enlarged labels exceed their cells. Verify other shared consumers in a separately scoped change.
- **Evidence:** `matrix.json`, `details.json`, `page-320-text200-hub.png`, `narrow-navigation-focus.png`, `narrow-horizontal-pan.png`, `case-keyboard.json`.
