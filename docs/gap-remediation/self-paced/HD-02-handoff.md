## Trace and label agreement

- **Automated:** `recognition-practice.test.tsx` parses the SVG path the practice renders with labels shown and the drawn landmark and ECG marker positions. The right ventricle has one peak per beat at RVSP, a falling “rapid fall” and a rising “up-sloping diastole”; the pulmonary artery has a notch minimum at its marker followed by the dicrotic wave, a falling diastole and a higher diastolic floor than the ventricle; the right atrium and its variant have peaks at a, c and v and troughs at x and y, a after P, c between QRS and T, and a taller than v; the wedge has only a and v peaks, a between QRS and T, v after T, and v taller than a; the variant is the reference moved by one constant to within 2 px of the wedge mean; the damped display has no landmarks, a narrower pulse, a shallower notch and a similar mean. Tolerance is a twentieth of a beat.
- **Cannot-interpret states:** “Cannot be named” is the reading only for the damped display, which reuses the authored overdamped challenge and draws no landmarks; on a clean tracing that reading gets “No display fault is drawn on this model tracing…”. The registry validator refuses a display-fault example that names a place, a hint or unlabelled description that names a reading, a comparison across axes, and an origin note that does not say it is not a patient recording.
- **Observations for faculty, unchanged:** the drawn P-to-a interval is about 100 ms for the right atrium and 300 ms for the wedge at the figure's one-second beat, against the atlas text's about 80 ms and 240 ms (order and delay hold, magnitudes differ); the ECG is one idealized sinus rhythm for every tracing; the atrial and ventricular pairs use different axes. Details and reviewer columns: [HD-02 faculty comparison sheet](HD-02-faculty-trace-comparison.md).
