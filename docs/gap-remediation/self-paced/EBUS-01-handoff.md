## Progress, storage and legacy records

- **Current record:** `ip-ebus-guided-self-paced-v1`. Fields: `version`, `lastLocation` (lesson, practice lab, practice case or integrated case), `visitedLessonIds`, `reviewedLessonIds`, `reviewLaterLessonIds`, `openedPracticeCaseIds`, `openedIntegratedCaseIds`, `updatedAt`. Writes happen on opening a lesson, lab or case, on finishing a lesson (reviewed), on undoing that, and on toggling Save for later. It holds no answer, correctness, explanation or hint use, help request, held image, acquisition, task completion or record submission; a value carrying any extra field fails to parse and is left as it is.
- **Legacy record** `ip-ebus-guided-v1`: no current module reads or writes it. Its parser and two pure transforms remain in `engine/progress.ts` as dormant compatibility; the structural test fails if any component or route imports that module or if anything but `selfPacedProgress.ts` and `engine/examination.ts` writes storage. The Overview says the record exists and is not used.
- **Examination drafts** `ip-ebus-guided-examination:<caseId>` are unchanged in shape and remain the record-task workspace; they no longer gain `firstSubmissions` entries.
- **No conversion of history:** old completions, first attempts, support requests, skill observations and completed cases are not turned into visited, reviewed or opened state, and no legacy location is used to resume.
- **Unreadable or unavailable storage:** an unreadable self-paced value is left byte for byte and nothing is written over it; unavailable storage saves nothing. Every page says so and stays open.
- **Stable identities kept:** lesson, activity and question ids (including the `-application-v2` ids and the historical image questions), case ids, `examinationKey`, `recordTaskKey`, the `/ebus-guided`, `/learn?section=`, `/practice` and `/assess` addresses.
- **Telemetry and accounts:** the module makes no analytics or account calls (grep of `components` and `engine` for `analytics`, `trackEvent`, `lifecycle`, `fetch(`: only `DecisionImage` fetching its own reference image).

## Legacy-progress implications

A learner with an earlier record sees the new course with no lessons marked and a note that the earlier record exists and is not used.
