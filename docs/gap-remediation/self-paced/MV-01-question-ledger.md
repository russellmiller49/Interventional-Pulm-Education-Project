## Removed administrative prompts and dormant compatibility

- Required confidence/first-attempt commitment, required end-of-case acknowledgement, completion submission, percentage result screens, and mandatory transfer submission are **removed from current entry paths**. They existed to record or award performance, not teach an additional concept.
- Retained legacy scoring, question-renderer, replay-parser and `CaseWorkflow` code is unreachable from public MV routes. Its historical tests remain when they check engine/data compatibility. It is not a parallel fellowship or course mode.
- No question removal creates a surrogate answer, prediction, bedside assessment, gas result, hold, intervention or successful run. A baseline is a snapshot of the actual initialized patient; a response requires the real action, interval and applicable validity checks.
- Remaining clinical content/source review and richer comparisons belong to subsequent MV slices. Technical verification of this conversion is not source or clinical approval.
