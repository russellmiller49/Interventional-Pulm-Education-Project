# EBUS-01 — question ledger (all 108 items)

Per-item disposition ledger for the multiple-choice questions of the EBUS guided module, drafted 2026-09-14 for the EBUS-01 self-paced conversion. Basis: a 108-item inventory read from the module content in worktree `claude/track-b-9-14` (HEAD `dea4e80d`): 78 lesson questions (26 lessons × question / observation / transfer slots) and 30 case questions (8 integrated `assessment-*` cases, 3 `practice-*` cases). Stems and options are quoted from that inventory. Explanations were not re-read for this ledger and are not quoted; the three G00 samples are reproduced from the [G00 ledger](question-ledger.md) with their dispositions unchanged.

**What EBUS-01 does to every item.** The mechanics are not a per-item decision: each of the 108 questions becomes optional reinforcement with **Hint**, **Show explanation before answering**, **Try again**, and **Continue without answering**. The `Disposition` column records the _content_ recommendation on top of that. `keep` needs no further action. Every other disposition (replace, combine, rewrite, remove) is a proposal — marked "proposed · pending EBUS faculty review" — that changes no question text, answer, source, unsafe flag or approval; the three G00 dispositions are reused verbatim and carry the same status. No clinical fact, source or review is asserted here that is not already in the content.

**How dispositions were applied.**

- `keep` — the alternatives are plausible confusions and the concept is named. Where one alternative is off-axis but the other carries the intended confusion, the item is kept and the weak alternative is noted under Friction.
- `replace` — proposed only where neither alternative competes with the correct explanation _and_ the same lesson's lab already shows the change (the G00 orientation-predict standard). The MCQ would give way to that comparison with an immediately available explanation.
- `combine` — the item restates its lesson's matching activity or a neighbouring question; fold it into that activity.
- `rewrite` — wording only, no clinical change; the note says what.
- `remove` — none proposed. Every item names a concept.

Lesson ↔ case repeats (for example `result-predict` / `differential-blood`) are cross-surface revisits by design and are kept.

**Notation.** `*` marks the correct option and `!` an option carrying an unsafe flag in the content; both are quoted from the inventory, not assigned here. `held (retained-acquisition)` items need a real held image before they can be answered; under EBUS-01 their explanation is readable without the image. `held` without a policy means the activity shows a held image but does not require one.

**Counts.** Disposition: keep 97 · replace 4 · combine 4 · rewrite 3 · remove 0 (108). Items with at least one unsafe-flagged option: 15 (11 lesson, 4 case). Retained-acquisition items: 14.
