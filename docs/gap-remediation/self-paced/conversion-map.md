Paths are repository-relative. `E` means educational gate/grade; `S` means simulation/data precondition; `M` means a predicate or workflow combines them. This is a conversion inventory, not a claim of completed conversion. Browser evidence is separately recorded in [G00-handoff](G00-handoff.md).

## Public entry and result coverage

Each base below has `page.tsx`, `learn/page.tsx`, `practice/page.tsx`, and `assess/page.tsx` under `src/app/[locale]/`. The locale prefix remains in URLs. “Public path” here includes existing draft/unlisted direct links; **it does not authorize listing or publication**. Results are rendered inside the named feature components, not a newly invented `/results` route. No separate `/challenge` route was found in these nine route families; Challenge is a mode on the existing paths.

## Predicates, persistence, and required separation

### Peripheral Imaging — PI-01 pilot

- **E:** `engine/caseStandard.ts::capstoneUnlocked` requires every Learn section; `capstoneStandard` requires all eight decisions, at least seven correct and every safety-critical decision correct. `components/ImagingCapstone.tsx` uses both, disables submission without a choice, retains first attempts, withholds the aggregate debrief until all decisions, and displays the standard in words. Removing digits alone would leave a grade.
- **M:** `engine/stageSession.ts::stepWorkDone` treats prediction/arrays of sorting answers alongside real `labGoalsMet`. `deriveStageProgress` stops at the first unperformed step. `components/stage/ImagingStageHost.tsx` has pending-choice/sort gates, real lab goals and snapshot actions. Separate unrestricted topic navigation/reveal from actual acquisition and comparison evidence. Do not fill `performedIds` or issue `SNAPSHOT` to simulate a skip.
- **S:** Preserve `engine/labGoalEvaluation.ts`, the actual acquisition/capture checks under `lib/`, and teaching model/source distinctions. `__tests__/acquisition-capture.test.ts` is protected.
- **Writes/consumers:** `engine/learnProgress.ts` (`writeImagingRecord`, `withFirstAttempt`, section completion, capstone-debrief timestamp); `components/usePeripheralImagingRecord.ts` reads current and v1 records; `content/pathwayResolver.ts::nextIncompleteImagingSection` and `components/hub/ImagingPathwayAccordion.tsx` derive the hub/Continue state from completed sections. Inventory both the migrated in-memory view and later whole-record writes. Existing v1 records must not become newly written graded records.
- **Target:** open all current cases and topics, provide direct explanations and optional retries, store only declared minimal navigation/review state. Keep the lesson layout, images and mechanisms. Convert the full route/result/storage path in PI-01, not just its first screen.

### EBUS Guided — EBUS-01

- **E:** `engine/progress.ts::assessmentReady` requires all `LESSONS`; `components/AssessPage.tsx` renders “Finish the guided course first” and a correct/answered first-response table. `QuestionBody.tsx` only renders `AnswerVerdict` when `committed` exists. `CasePlayer.tsx` supplies mode-specific submission, repair and debrief behavior.
- **M:** `LessonHost.tsx::advance` and `disabled` combine `pendingQuestion`, `interactionDone`, `missingImage`, `labDone` and `holdRequested`. An unsafe choice leads to “Revise this response” and clears the in-session answer instead of allowing onward navigation. The outline disables unreached activities. Preserve the useful safety explanation, remove forced repair as a navigation requirement.
- **S:** `engine/evidence.ts`, `engine/examination.ts`, retained-image checks and `recordLinkedObservation` protect frame/session identity, actual acquisition, decoded/held images and supported view. Keep these protections for any claimed acquisition; offer example/reveal or leave when acquisition is unavailable. Do not manufacture a frame or observation.
- **Writes/consumers:** `ip-ebus-guided-v1` in `engine/progress.ts` includes `firstAttempts`, `supportRequests`, linked observations, lesson/case completions and last lesson. `updateRecord`, `firstAttempt`, `recordSupportRequest`, `completeLesson`, `completeCase`, and `useCourseRecord.ts` need a current-session write audit. Stop assistance surveillance as well as score displays. Keep real historical acquisition records unchanged.
