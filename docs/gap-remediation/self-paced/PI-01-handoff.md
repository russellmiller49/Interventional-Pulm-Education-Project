## Progress, storage and legacy records

- **Current record:** `ip-peripheral-imaging-self-paced-v1` (`engine/selfPacedProgress.ts`, strict zod). Fields: `version`, `lastLocation` (section, practice case or integrated case), `visitedSectionIds`, `reviewedSectionIds`, `reviewLaterSectionIds`, `openedPracticeCaseIds`, `openedIntegratedCaseIds`, `updatedAt`. Writes happen on opening a section or case, finishing a section, and toggling Save for review. It holds no answer, correctness, explanation or help use, performed step, capture or readout; a record carrying any extra field fails to parse.
- **Legacy records** `ip-peripheral-imaging-v2` (completed sections, last section, first attempts, capstone debrief time) and `ip-peripheral-imaging-v1` (draft): no current module reads or writes them. The writers `writeImagingRecord`, `readImagingRecord`, `withFirstAttempt`, `withSectionVisited`, `withSectionCompleted` and `withCapstoneDebriefViewed` were removed, and the record hook deleted. The pure parsers `parseImagingRecord`, `parseLegacyImagingRecord` and `migrateImagingRecordFromV1` remain as dormant compatibility so the bytes stay interpretable; a structural test fails if any Imaging module or route imports them, or if anything but `selfPacedProgress.ts` writes storage.
- **No conversion of history:** legacy completion and first decisions are not turned into visited, reviewed or opened state, and no legacy location is used to resume.
- **Unreadable or unavailable storage:** an unreadable self-paced value is left byte-for-byte and nothing is written over it; unavailable storage saves nothing. The hub and Learn landing then say this browser is not saving, and everything stays open.
- **Stable identities kept:** section, activity and question ids (including the `-v2` ids), item ids `capstone:<case>` and `practice:<case>`, `CAPSTONE_ACTIVITY_ID`, and the `/peripheral-imaging/assess` address.
- **Telemetry and accounts:** the Imaging components make no analytics or network calls (grep of `components` and `engine` for `analytics`, `trackEvent`, `lifecycle` and `fetch(`), and Imaging progress has no account sync.

## Legacy-progress implications

- A learner with an existing v2 or v1 record keeps those bytes on the device, but the course no longer shows "worked through" chips, their first decisions or their capstone result, and the hub recommends from the new record only — for them it starts at section one until they open or finish sections. Showing legacy completion as "opened" would be a separate owner decision; it was not done because the owner asked that old progress not be turned into new self-paced progress.
- Nothing deletes, migrates or rewrites legacy data, and the read-only parsers keep it interpretable for any future, separately authorized reader.
- The new record is module-local; no cross-module progress service, account sync or telemetry was added.
