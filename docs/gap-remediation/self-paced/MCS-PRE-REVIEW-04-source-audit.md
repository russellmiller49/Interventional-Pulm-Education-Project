## What the original source display got wrong

1. The hub bypassed the opened-first helper and put unopened primary guidelines above opened secondary evidence. The repaired hub uses the same six-tier ordering as lessons/cases: opened primary, opened secondary, unopened primary, unopened secondary, model provenance, authoring provenance.
2. “Current safety-review flags,” a purported July 19 check and “Labeling sources last checked” implied verification inconsistent with the declared unopened FDA records. Device-profile and release-gate prose repeated that implication. Repairs explicitly distinguish registry history from verified current status and label each notice as unopened. Gate completion flags and dates are unchanged.
3. The MCS-03-10 hold was inside a closed claim disclosure, and the component returned nothing when a section/case had no mapped claim. Independent browser observations found no visible hold in Sections 1, 4, 8 or 9. The repaired hub, preparatory references, lesson task, cases and Studio expose the named **NOT REVIEWED / source-owner review required** hold without opening references.
4. The old-source narrative was too absolute: Section 3's aggregate footer already included Cardiosave references. “Only named basis” was corrected; the audit distinguishes direct lesson/case ordering from wider collected references.
5. The RP Flex crosswalk named a registered page as though it had been read. Its identity is established by the registry title/URL, but the repair explicitly says the page was not opened. No current product/performance/market/software conclusion was added.

The supplied Word syntheses remain available as authoring provenance, not independent evidence, and last in ordered citation lists. Unopened guidelines, HM3 instructions and FDA records were not silently promoted to “read.” The two-source conflict in the 2021 textbook remains a conflict; no averaging or updated clinical specification was invented. MCS-03-01 through -10 and OD-01 through -07 remain unresolved.
