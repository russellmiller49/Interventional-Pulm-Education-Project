## Model and source assumptions

- **HD-08's fault is taken to be the one the repository authors**: `artifact: 'false-wedge'` with
  the tip at an occluding position and the balloon down. Four independent places in the code say
  so (the case definition, its short title, the intervention description, and the reducer's own
  withdrawal message), and the engine's channel derivation draws PA morphology for exactly that
  artifact. The repair makes the _presentation_ agree with that authoring; it does not decide that
  this is the right fault to teach. **That remains R1.**
- No new catheter dimension, balloon volume, inflation time, withdrawal distance, drug target,
  diagnostic cutoff or acceptance percentage was introduced.
- The simulation safety notice deliberately describes the simulation, not a device. No manufacturer
  alarm was invented.
- `wedgeEpisodeCount` is a simulation bookkeeping value, not a clinical count, and is never shown.
