## What IC1 asked for, and what these drafts do

The walkthrough: "Each case is a single decision tied to one section … I expected one patient
followed through the procedure: choose the projection from the CT, lose the lesion to atelectasis,
decide between DTS and CBCT, confirm the sampling window, document the dose. Two or three unfolding
cases with four or five linked decisions each" [S: PDF p.48]. The pack narrows that to two optional
teaching cases with four or five decisions each, built from existing objectives, with no
examination [S: ledger IC1; `04_PI_CASE_AND_MEDIA_DRAFTS.md`].

- **Connected case 1**, "The nodule that faded": planning a projection, losing the nodule to
  probable dependent atelectasis, choosing the acquisition, centring the spin, and reading the
  sampling window. It follows the walkthrough's suggested arc for its first four steps.
- **Connected case 2**, "Two machines, one record": reading a reconstruction that may carry prior
  CT content, using a mobile CBCT volume without a validated integration, re-localizing after a
  sample, protecting the team during the repeat spin, and writing the record.

The cases feel connected because **evidence accumulates**: each decision starts from what the
previous one established. The learner is not scored.

**Owner decision (OD4-07, 2026-09-22).** Both case concepts and the `connected-*` namespace are
approved as drafts, with one change, applied below: Case 1 D3 no longer states that CBCT is the
correct modality. It asks which acquisition best answers the unresolved 3D question given the
room's platform capabilities. Placement is still open. The storyboards remain AI-authored and not
clinically reviewed ([owner-decisions.md](owner-decisions.md#recorded-owner-decisions-2026-09-22)).

## Shared design (both cases)

**Identity** [T]. New namespace, reusing no live id: cases `connected-1` and `connected-2`,
decision items `connected-1:d1` … `connected-2:d5` (namespace approved, OD4-07). Nothing is
keyed `case-N` or `capstone:`. Where they live (a "Connected cases" group on the Integrated cases
page, or a page of their own) is still open.

**Self-paced behaviour on every decision** [T; following the pack and PI-01]:

| Behaviour       | How it works                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Case so far     | Each decision opens with a short **"Case so far"** card holding the facts established before it: the story's facts, **never the learner's earlier answers**. A learner who jumps straight to decision 4 has what they need. |
| Explanation     | "Show the explanation" is available before answering, as on every current case.                                                                                                                                             |
| Retry           | An answer can be checked as often as the learner likes. Changing it is always allowed.                                                                                                                                      |
| Skip / jump     | "Continue to decision N+1" is always available, answered or not. A decision outline lists all five and opens any of them. No decision requires an earlier one.                                                              |
| Score           | None. No final score, no count of best-supported answers, no completion claim. The last screen is a short recap of what the five decisions connected.                                                                       |
| Safety emphasis | Decisions marked **safety emphasis** use the Prompt 03 IC4 wording: teaching emphasis only, no weight, nothing recorded, no restriction [S: P03 handoff, IC4].                                                              |
| Storage         | Nothing is written except, at most, that the case was opened, matching PI-01's practice-case rule [I: `ImagingCaseActivity`].                                                                                               |

## Connected case 1 · "The nodule that faded"

**Context** [T]. An adult is having navigation bronchoscopy under general anesthesia, lying supine,
for an 18-mm part-solid nodule in the posterior left lung (the course's teaching nodule). A
catheter-based navigation platform is in use. The room's C-arm can take fluoroscopy, a DTS sweep
over a limited arc (this system's DTS display can blend in the planning CT; premise), and a CBCT
spin. A radial EBUS probe is available. There is no robot and no
augmented-fluoroscopy overlay in this case, which keeps the evidence sources few.

**Objectives touched** [M: `OBJECTIVES`]: `optimize` (D1), `choose` and `verify` (D2), `dts` and
`cbct` (D3), `cbct` (D4), `verify` (D5).

**Evidence arc**: planning CT and projection → a change in the patient → choice of acquisition →
acquisition set-up → tool relationship in the volume.

## Connected case 2 · "Two machines, one record"

**Context** [T]. A different adult is having robotic navigation bronchoscopy under general
anesthesia for a peripheral nodule. The robotic platform can acquire a DTS sweep, and its
reconstruction can draw on the planning CT (premise). A mobile CBCT scanner has been brought into
the room; the room, table and scanner were commissioned together (premise). **This room has no
validated integration** between the scanner and the navigation platform, either for target update
or for a live overlay (premise). Platforms of this general kind are described in the literature:
robotic navigation with integrated tomosynthesis [S: FRONTIER abstract, PMID 38923084], and robotic
bronchoscopy integrated with mobile CBCT [S: CONFIRM abstract, PMID 41698810]. No product is named or
implied.

**Objectives touched** [M]: `dts` (D1), `cbct` (D2), `verify` (D3), `protect` (D4, D5).

the patient changes after a sample → a repeat spin → the whole-procedure record.
