## 2. How items are classified

The classification is about the **decision the item teaches**, not about whether today's wording
happens to narrate the finding. Whether the current wording asks the learner to read something off
an image is recorded separately.

- **A · image-essential.** The intended decision depends on a visual finding: the skill is reading
  the image, whether or not the image is on screen today.
- **B · text-scenario appropriate.** The decision is reasoning from stated context: evidence
  endpoints, a control's effect, a safety or communication choice, or a record. An image would be
  decoration or would restate the text.
- **C · ambiguous, owner decision.** The item or its section could reasonably mean either. Usually
  the section's outcome names a visual skill but the item was written as reasoning from described
  cues.

**Asset status** uses four values:

- **existing and eligible**: already in Git and published by the module with recorded provenance,
  and honest for the stated role _as a teaching model_. That means `public/peripheral-imaging/`:
  CT-derived thorax and atlas with an **authored** nodule, DTS projections, the sampling-window GLB,
  procedural suite geometry, and SVG teaching panels [I: `public/peripheral-imaging/README.md`,
  `manifest.json`]. It never makes the image patient evidence.
- **existing but rights uncertain**: a file exists but its rights basis is not recorded in anything
  read. The teaching CT under the model is **in Git and published**, but the files read name its
  source (`target_clean_ct.nrrd`, "existing FluoroView teaching CT") without stating a licence or
  consent basis [I: `docs/peripheral-imaging/slicer-assets.md`]. That is flagged once as **O-CT**
  in [owner-decisions.md](owner-decisions.md#od4-03--authentic-clinical-media-policy) and not repeated
  on every row.
- **model/simulator only**: honest evidence can come only from the course's teaching models, or a
  new model build. That is fine for a mechanism, and never evidence of a patient finding.
- **absent**: nothing suitable exists in the repository or in the Local-Data locations searched (see
  [README](README.md#sources-actually-read-whole-packet); every filename hit was a screenshot of this
  module).

**Two hard rules carried from Prompt 01** [S: PI-FELLOW-03 handoff "Preserved"; I:
`fixedExampleEvidence`]. A fixed example's image must either depict the question or say that it is
the section's teaching model. A stored frame carries acquisition, not display. Every proposal below
keeps both.
