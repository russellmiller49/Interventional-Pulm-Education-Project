# Curriculum — Rigid Bronchoscopy Technique Videos

> **Status: PLANNING / DRAFT.** No media has been generated. Higgsfield is
> configured but not yet authenticated. This curriculum plans the learner-facing
> structure; every clip referenced is DRAFT until physician review and must not
> appear on the production learner route before sign-off.

**This module SUPPLEMENTS supervised simulation and clinical instruction. It does
NOT independently credential a learner to perform rigid bronchoscopy.** Skills are
demonstrated on an adult airway **training manikin** and in **synthetic** airway
visualizations; any generated intraluminal view is labeled **"Synthetic procedural
visualization"** and is never presented as real footage.

Nine lessons (canonical ids): `positioning`, `oral-entry`, `glottic-passage`,
`tracheal-advancement`, `scope-manipulation`, `mainstem-direction`, `apple-coring`,
`unsafe-mechanics`, `integrated-sequence`.

## Panel convention (applies to every lesson)

Lessons pair an **external panel** (generated hand/barrel/manikin mechanics) with a
**synthetic panel** (validated 3D model/diagram of the internal consequence). The
synthetic panel carries the persistent **"Synthetic procedural visualization"**
label and is never real footage. Exact internal anatomy is driven by validated
assets, not by free generation. All on-screen text is added deterministically in
postproduction.

## Lesson 1 — Positioning (`positioning`)

- **Objective (measurable):** Given a setup frame, the learner correctly
  identifies all four baseline elements (supine manikin, operator at head of bed,
  aligned airway axis, dental protection placed) before instrumentation.
- **Approx duration:** 3–5 min.
- **Safety statement:** Positioning precedes instrumentation; dental protection is
  placed and is never a fulcrum; no advancement occurs.
- **Key movement rule:** Establish alignment and operator position first; the
  barrel does not approach the mouth until the setup is stable.
- **Common error:** Instrumenting before alignment or dental protection is ready.
- **Retrieval:** (1) Name the four baseline setup elements. (2) When may the
  barrel first approach the mouth?
- **Panels:** External only; any orientation aid is a validated static diagram.

## Lesson 2 — Oral entry (`oral-entry`)

- **Objective:** The learner demonstrates a controlled **midline** oral
  introduction of the beveled tip with dental protection maintained and no incisor
  levering.
- **Approx duration:** 4–6 min.
- **Safety statement:** Slow, midline, controlled entry; dental protection present;
  stop if the view is not maintained.
- **Key movement rule:** Introduce the beveled tip in the midline; the barrel stays
  straight and constant in diameter.
- **Common error:** Levering on the incisors or off-midline/forceful entry.
- **Retrieval:** (1) Where does the barrel contact for support instead of the
  teeth? (2) What triggers stopping during entry?
- **Panels:** External mechanics; optional validated oropharyngeal diagram.

## Lesson 3 — Cord passage (`glottic-passage`)

- **Objective:** The learner performs the timed ~90° rotation to align the barrel's
  narrower dimension with the glottic opening, passes under continuous
  visualization, and returns to the working orientation.
- **Approx duration:** 5–7 min.
- **Safety statement:** Slow, atraumatic, continuously visualized passage with
  tooth protection and no forceful advancement; stop/reposition if the view is lost.
- **Key movement rule:** Rotate ~90° immediately before passage; **return to the
  working orientation** after controlled passage.
- **Common error:** Forcing passage, advancing without a view, or not de-rotating.
- **Retrieval:** (1) Why rotate ~90° before the glottis? (2) What is the next step
  immediately after passage?
- **Panels:** Synthetic internal (labeled) + external rotation mechanics.

## Lesson 4 — Tracheal advancement (`tracheal-advancement`)

- **Objective:** The learner advances midline within the trachea, confirming the
  lumen under direct visualization before each increment, and settles to a neutral
  distal-tracheal position.
- **Approx duration:** 3–5 min.
- **Safety statement:** Advance only with the lumen in view; smooth, unhurried,
  barrel rigid; stop if the lumen is not clearly seen.
- **Key movement rule:** Lumen in view before every advance; settle neutral before
  side selection.
- **Common error:** Advancing without a clear lumen or drifting off midline.
- **Retrieval:** (1) What must be confirmed before each advance? (2) What is the
  handoff position into mainstem selection?
- **Panels:** External advance + validated lumen-view reference.

## Lesson 5 — Movement map (`scope-manipulation`)

- **Objective:** For each isolated proximal movement (advance, withdraw, CW/CCW
  rotation, proximal-barrel left/right, head adjustment, combined), the learner
  predicts the distal-tip response before it is revealed.
- **Approx duration:** 6–9 min.
- **Safety statement:** One variable at a time; barrel rigid; no advance beyond loss
  of visualization; teeth never a fulcrum.
- **Key movement rule:** Change one variable at a time; predict, then confirm.
- **Common error:** Combining movements or over-rotating.
- **Retrieval:** (1) Which way does the distal tip align when the proximal barrel
  moves to the patient's right? (2) Does an isolated head adjustment guarantee
  mainstem entry?
- **Panels:** External proximal movement → prediction → validated distal-response
  panel (labeled). Side-specific distal panels are separately validated, never
  mirrored.

## Lesson 6 — Mainstem direction (`mainstem-direction`, prototype)

- **Objective:** The learner states and demonstrates that aligning the distal tip
  toward a target mainstem requires moving the head and proximal barrel toward the
  **opposite** patient-anatomical side, and that advancement follows only after
  visualization and alignment.
- **Approx duration:** 6–9 min.
- **Safety statement:** External movement shown slowly with the barrel rigid; no
  incisor levering; no blind advance; head movement alone does not guarantee entry.
- **Key movement rule:** Proximal barrel and head move away from the target side;
  the distal tip aligns toward it; advance only after confirmed alignment.
- **Common error:** Levering on the teeth, advancing before alignment, or assuming
  head movement alone directs the tip.
- **Retrieval:** (1) Target = left mainstem: which way do the head and proximal
  barrel move? (2) What must precede advancement?
- **Panels:** External (generated) + validated synthetic internal consequence
  (labeled). Right-side content is separately validated, never a flipped left clip.

## Lesson 7 — Apple-coring mechanics (`apple-coring`)

- **Objective:** The learner demonstrates a short controlled axial advance with slow
  rotation that engages a simplified intraluminal lesion while the normal airway
  wall remains stationary, then pauses, removes the fragment, suctions, and
  reinspects.
- **Approx duration:** 5–7 min.
- **Safety statement:** Short controlled advance, never a forceful thrust; if the
  wall moves with the lesion, stop; no blind advance, no barrel bending, no gore.
- **Key movement rule:** Short advance + controlled rotation; wall stationary; then
  pause → withdraw → remove → suction → recenter → reinspect.
- **Common error:** Forceful/deep advance, uncontrolled rotation, or leaving a
  mobile fragment.
- **Retrieval:** (1) What indicates you should stop during coring? (2) What are the
  post-coring recovery steps?
- **Panels:** External mechanics + separately produced synthetic internal mechanics
  (persistent label).

## Lesson 8 — Unsafe-mechanics recognition (`unsafe-mechanics`)

- **Objective:** The learner recognizes each unsafe pattern early and selects the
  correct recovery (Stop → Withdraw → Suction → Re-establish anatomy → Reassess the
  tissue plane).
- **Approx duration:** 5–7 min.
- **Safety statement:** Every example freezes before injury; no harm is depicted;
  synthetic bleeding, if shown, is clinically reviewed and stops before graphic.
- **Key movement rule:** On any warning sign, stop and run the recovery sequence.
- **Common error:** Continuing the motion after a warning sign appears.
- **Retrieval:** (1) State the five-step recovery sequence. (2) Name two signs that
  require immediate stopping.
- **Panels:** External and/or validated synthetic internal (labeled), with the
  identical recovery overlay.

## Lesson 9 — Integrated sequence (`integrated-sequence`)

- **Objective:** The learner orders the full sequence correctly — positioning →
  oral entry → cord passage → tracheal advancement → movement/mainstem selection →
  (task) — and names the visualization checkpoint before each advance.
- **Approx duration:** 6–8 min.
- **Safety statement:** Assembled from single-movement clips; every transition
  preserves continuous visualization and rigid-barrel discipline; no step combines
  multiple major movements in one generation.
- **Key movement rule:** One major movement per step; confirm visualization before
  advancing at every transition.
- **Common error:** Skipping the visualization checkpoint between steps.
- **Retrieval:** (1) Put the six phases in order. (2) What is confirmed at every
  transition before advancing?
- **Panels:** Assembled external + validated synthetic panels from the prior
  lessons; no new uncontrolled generation of internal anatomy.

## Assessment

  `mainstem-direction` freeze frame (no generated text; question added in
  postproduction).
- **Manikin practice checklist:** hands-on drill on the training manikin between the
  movement map and apple-coring lessons, verified by supervising faculty.
- **Faculty assessment checklist:** clinical sign-off per `clinical-review-checklist.md`;
  only the physician owner or a designated faculty reviewer may set
