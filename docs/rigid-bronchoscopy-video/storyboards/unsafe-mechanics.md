This lesson **supplements** supervised simulation and clinical instruction and
does **not** credential a learner to perform rigid bronchoscopy.

---

## Objective

The learner recognizes each unsafe-mechanics pattern early and selects the correct
recovery action before injury.

## Safety statement

**Every unsafe example FREEZES before any serious injury.** No clip depicts harm.
Bleeding, if shown, is a clearly synthetic, clinically reviewed illustration that
stops before obscuring the plane in a graphic way. These are recognition drills,
not demonstrations of how to injure tissue.

## Key movement rule (recovery sequence)

On recognizing any unsafe pattern: **Stop → Withdraw → Suction → Re-establish
anatomy → Reassess the tissue plane.** This deterministic overlay accompanies
every example.

## Common error

Continuing the motion after a warning sign appears instead of stopping and
recovering.

## Left/right orientation check

These are recognition drills, not side-specific maneuvers. Record camera location
per shot and keep an asymmetrical landmark in frame to detect mirroring. If any
example incidentally shows a side, describe it in patient anatomical terms. See
`left-right-orientation-standard.md`.

## External-view vs synthetic-view relationship

Examples may use an external panel, a validated synthetic internal panel, or both.
Any internal panel is labeled **"Synthetic procedural visualization"** and never
presented as real footage. The recovery overlay is identical across panels.

  Re-establish anatomy / Reassess the tissue plane.**
