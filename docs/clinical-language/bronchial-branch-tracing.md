The brief asked for about 30%. The screens where the scope is driven with the map reach it (25% to 33% less). The planning screens are 8% to 17% shorter, and the screens that read the CT at a fork about 5% to 8%. What is left on those screens is the method's own teaching and the facts a choice is made from; taking more would remove content, which is the owner's call. The candidates are listed under "Clinical-review queue".

Two of the cuts are in what is shown, not in wording:

1. **The fork's CT table and its one-line pattern are shown only while the CT (or a reconstruction) is the middle pane.** With the map in the middle pane the fork's card keeps its name and what the lesson says there. This is most of the saving on the map screens.
2. **"Turning the CT is your choice" is said at the first fork of a trip that asks anything, not at every fork.** The Check my turn button beside the CT says it after that.
