## Unchanged and deferred work

Practice retains its case renderer, first-response policy and paired-lesson links. Assess retains independent responses, delayed non-safety explanations, immediate critical-safety refusal and its existing Learn prerequisite gate; its heading now derives the item count. Reference retains its registries, sources, local-policy caveats and atlas, with larger anchor targets. Both entry URLs use the same course hub.

Optional advanced extensions, new abnormal images, a new physiology engine, patient-specific dose/device advice, releases, access changes, deployment and merging are outside this implementation. Faculty/rights approval and novice human usability validation remain pending; they are not implementation failures or completed validation.

The new personal-record report adds an evidence requirement, so `honest-report` is version 2. The equivalent five-controls pilot remains version 2. Other section versions remain unchanged. Historical completions and first responses are retained. Only a finished survey stores its examination snapshot; absent prior snapshots are reported as unavailable.
