# Sponsorship policy

**Status: draft, written by an AI authoring assistant for the owner to revise and adopt. It is
not in force and has not been agreed with any sponsor.**

This is the site's policy for a teaching module that a company pays for. It was written for the
first such module, Medical Thoracoscopy, sponsored by Richard Wolf. Other modules are not
sponsored and say so.

## The arrangement in one paragraph

A sponsor funds a module. The owner decides what the module teaches. The sponsor checks the facts
about its own devices. Learners are told who paid, on every page of the module.

## What the owner controls

- What is taught, in what order, and how.
- Which sources are used and how they are weighed.
- What a simulation shows, and what it says it does not show.
- Every comparison between devices, techniques or approaches.
- Whether and when the module is published, corrected or withdrawn.

## What the sponsor may review

| The sponsor checks                               | The sponsor does not decide        |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Device descriptions, dimensions and part numbers | Clinical teaching                  |
| Which products are available in which market     | Which sources are cited            |
| How its trademarks are written                   | Comparative claims                 |
| Whether a depiction of its product is accurate   | What learners are shown about risk |

A sponsor's fact-check is recorded per claim, with the reviewer's name and the date, in a lane
separate from clinical review. It never implies clinical approval. Feedback outside that scope is
read and may be declined.

## What learners are told

- A disclosure on the module's overview, on every page of the module and beside its sources.
- The disclosure names the sponsor, says the owner keeps editorial control, and says what the
  sponsor reviewed.
- The wording is the owner's. Nothing is displayed until the owner has approved it.

## What the sponsor receives

- No data about any learner.
- No tracking added for the sponsor.
- If a report is agreed, it uses only a data source that already exists and is already authorised
  for that purpose, and contains aggregate figures only.

## How the sponsor's products are shown

- Described, not praised. No superlatives and no unsourced comparisons.
- Original artwork only. A model is labelled "Educational rendering from published dimensions"
  until it is built from the manufacturer's own CAD.
- No logo or stylised wordmark until the sponsor licenses it in writing.
- No competitor's images. Where other instruments or approaches are in use, the module says so
  briefly, neutrally and with a source.
- The sponsor's images and documents are not reproduced. Facts are cited.

An automated check refuses promotional wording in learner-facing text. It supplements a person
reading the text. It does not replace one.

## Conflicts of interest

The owner records, for the module: who sponsors it, what was provided (funding, equipment,
documents, media), and any other relationship between the sponsor and the people who wrote or
reviewed the module. The record is kept by the owner and summarised in the disclosure.

## Ending or correcting

- The owner may correct or withdraw any part of a module at any time, without the sponsor's
  agreement.
- If the sponsorship ends, the disclosure stays for as long as the sponsored material does, and
  says when the sponsorship ended.
- If the sponsor withdraws a permission, the affected material is removed or replaced.

## Not covered here

The agreement between the owner and the sponsor: ownership of the models, licences to marks and
media, review turnaround, and any reporting obligations. Those are the owner's to settle and are
kept outside this repository.
