judgment the assistant cannot give. Nothing here says the gate is passed, and a green test is not
clinical approval.

## What the gate found, and what was repaired here

- **The lesson widened the page on a phone.** At 390 px it was 466 px wide and at 320 px wider still:
  every grid in the lesson and the pane sized its one column to its widest content, and the survey's
  note, a table of choices, cannot be narrower than its choices. Repaired: each grid's column may be
  narrower than its content, and the note scrolls sideways in its own box, as a data table may; the
  page never does.
- **The site blanks every page when a browser refuses storage.** The site's theme provider, outside
  this module, reads storage without a guard. The module's own record handles refusal (checked with
  its own key refused). Raised as a separate task; not repaired here.
