The gate is the owner's to call, and it has not been passed. This packet records, criterion by
criterion, what was shown, how, and what was not. A result is **MET** only where a check or a
measurement shows it, **PARTIAL** where some of what the criterion asks is shown and some is not,
**NOT MET AS SPECIFIED** where what was built is not what the criterion names, **NOT TESTED** where a
measurement was not taken, and **NOT RUN** where a check needs people, a device or a judgment the
assistant cannot give. A green test is not clinical, manufacturer or rights approval, and fuzzing is
evidence, never a proof.

## What the independent review changed

The first version of this packet overstated several criteria. The independent review reproduced the
evidence (48 focused suites and 613 tests, 20 of 20 browser checks, the 10,000-seed real-proxy fuzz,
the production build, the same full-suite and Storybook failures as `main`) and corrected the
states. The second column below is that correction; the third is where each criterion stands after
this repair. Where a repair strengthened the evidence, the state is still the owner's and the next
sanity re-review's to confirm.

**What the spike proves.** One contact framework serves the telescope and the forceps: the jaws may
touch what a scenario authorises while the telescope and the forceps' shaft may not; a pivot that
would sweep the forceps into the lung is refused with the part named; withdrawal is never refused
by a surface (with the jaws closed). After R8 the authorisation is region by region.

**What it does not prove.** Contact with the costal pleura; a clinically chosen target; contact while
the anatomy moves; anything about tissue. No region is claimed safe to touch.

## Public exposure, as it stands

Recorded, not approved (rights register, `exposure`; OD-13):

- **Device models.** The twelve instrument models and their manifest are committed on the pushed
  branches from pull request 296 on; the repository is public, so they can be downloaded from GitHub
  without signing in. They are not on `main`, not uploaded and not served by the production site.
- **Anatomy-derived records.** Twelve numeric records under `content/data/anatomy/` (listed in the
  asset ledger's `bundledRecords`) are committed on the pushed branches from pull request 300 on and
  are compiled into the module's page code. The anatomy models and the collision proxies are not in
  the repository and have not been uploaded.
- **The production path.** No anatomy file can go to the module asset storage while
  R-ANATOMY-SEGMENTATION is unresolved, so a production build has neither the 3D views nor the cut,
  whose geometry is the proxies; reading and navigation work, and the spatial controls say why they
  cannot act (S7, corrected).
