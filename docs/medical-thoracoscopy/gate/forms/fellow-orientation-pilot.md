# Form — fellows' orientation pilot

Blank. For the prototype gate's owner-run item: three to five fellows grasp the inverted mapping,
that the telescope's tip goes the other way to the hand. The owner runs it; nothing here is a
test of the fellow, and nothing the form records is shown to them.

**Keep the filled forms out of the repository.** They record observations of people: keep them
in the owner's local data (`medical_thoracoscopy/gate/pilot/`). Use a code (F1 to F5), never a
name, and record no institution, date of birth or anything else that identifies the fellow.
Consent follows the owner's own institution's practice; this form does not settle it.
