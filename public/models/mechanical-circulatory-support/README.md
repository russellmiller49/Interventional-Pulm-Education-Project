# Mechanical Circulatory Support teaching models

Original, neutral educational geometry generated by `scripts/generate-mcs-models.mjs`.

- `cutaway-heart-aorta.glb`: simplified heart, aorta, pulmonary artery, and aortic-valve landmark.
- `iabp-path.glb`: neutral catheter and balloon path.
- `impella-path.glb`: neutral transvalvular microaxial-pump path.
- `lvad-path.glb`: neutral apical inflow, pump, and ascending-aortic outflow path.

These assets do not reproduce manufacturer trade dress, dimensions, console interfaces, or implantation guidance. They are explanatory teaching facsimiles, not clinical planning models. Generated assets are project-owned and follow the repository license.
